In a plot twist even the most dramatic Bravo producer couldn't dream up, former 'Real Housewives of Beverly Hills' firebrand Brandi Glanville is voluntarily having her face TAKEN OFF. Yes, you read that correctly. The 53-year-old reality TV veteran declared on her podcast this week that she’s undergoing what she chillingly termed an “exploratory surgery” where a doctor will, in her own unforgettable words, 'do a power wash under my skin.' Forget Botox parties—this is next-level Beverly Hills body horror.

The cause? Glanville claims two distinct, moving spots beneath her skin that could be 'fungal balls or something alive.' She’s convinced it’s alive because, as she whispered with palpable dread, 'it moves when it wants.' The image she paints is pure sci-fi: a parasitic interloper making a home in her famous face. Dr. Carlo Henrado, the brave soul taking on this cinematic procedure, reportedly told her he aims to 'cut the head off the beast, the snake.' The price for this facial exorcism? Whatever insurance won't cover, plus a cool $5,000 out of pocket. A bargain for banishing a face-dwelling beast, we suppose.

But the saga of Brandi’s face is a winding, scandal-strewn road. She first pointed the finger at Bravo for her disfigurement, then doctors suspected a 'parasite that jumps around her face.' Others dismissed it as mere 'stress-induced edema.' The plot thickened further when she blamed her 20-year-old, ruptured breast implants for leaking silicone into her lymph nodes. A benign facial tumor diagnosis followed. Now, we’ve arrived at the face-removal chapter. Glanville described the upcoming ordeal as 'more intensive' than a facelift, involving knives, a power washer, and a platelet-rich plasma spin of her own blood—a concoction she joked, 'We might not want my blood at this point.'

The entire spectacle has unfolded like a reality TV season finale, complete with Instagram teasers. Last month, she shared footage of an ultrasound, claiming doctors 'saw something alive' and 'moving.' Her desperation peaked with the threat: 'If I don’t find a doctor to cut this out of my face, I’m just going to do it myself.' Thankfully, cooler heads—and a surgeon’s scalpel—have prevailed. Whether this is a tragic medical mystery or the most extreme storyline in Housewives history remains to be seen. One thing is certain: in the world of Brandi Glanville, the drama is always skin deep.
