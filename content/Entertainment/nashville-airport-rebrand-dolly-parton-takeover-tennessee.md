Move over, generic travel hubs, and make way for the rhinestone runway! In a move so fabulous it could only belong to her, Tennessee is staging a full-scale, sequin-studded takeover of Nashville International Airport, aiming to rechristen it in honor of the late, great Dolly Parton. SalaciousNews has learned that just days after the world wept for the loss of the Queen of Country, Governor Bill Lee and airport bigwigs unveiled a plan to give BNA the ultimate glow-up: a new moniker that screams Dolly from the tarmac to the terminal.

Governor Lee, likely dreaming of his own coat of many colors, gushed that the renaming would create ‘a lasting tribute to her indelible legacy.’ He painted a picture of weary travelers being greeted not by sterile signs, but by the ‘enduring legacy of Dolly’s music, generosity, faith, and kindness.’ Can you imagine? Trading bland airport announcements for a recording of ‘Jolene’ as you deplane? The camp! The glory! The sheer, unadulterated Tennessee pizazz!

The proposal, set to be rubber-stamped at an airport authority meeting next month, is the bureaucratic equivalent of a standing ovation. It comes on the heels of a massive public petition boasting over 160,000 signatures—proof that the people’s will is as powerful as Dolly’s wigs. A Nashville council member and a state rep have already thrown their support behind the idea, making this political smooth sailing smoother than Dolly’s high notes.

This isn't just a name change; it's a statement. It’s Tennessee declaring to the world that its heart, its humor, and its hair are forever shaped by a woman from a Smoky Mountain cabin who dared to dream bigger than her britches. The airport, a gateway to Music City, will transform into a permanent shrine to her philanthropy (hello, Imagination Library), her business savvy (bows to Dollywood), and her inimitable sparkle. In a world of beige bureaucracy, they’re painting it neon and bedazzling the heck out of it. Because if anyone deserves to have her name on a building where millions embark on journeys, it’s the woman who taught us all how to fly. Fasten your seatbelts, folks; it’s about to get a whole lot more colorful around here.
