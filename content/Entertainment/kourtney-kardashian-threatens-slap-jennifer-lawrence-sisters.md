'I said to my mom, "OK, she thinks she's one of my sisters, I'll f--king slap the s--t out of her,"' Kourtney confessed to pal Simon Huck, dripping with the same venom she once reserved for sister Kim. She even dared Lawrence to come over, promising a showdown 'Kourtney and Kim style.' The drama unfolded after Lawrence, who bizarrely claimed to feel 'like one of your sisters,' criticized Kourtney's penchant for making grand life announcements. 'Everything has to be an announcement,' Lawrence had whined, referencing Kourtney's declarations about not wearing outfits or owning a TV. But the Lemme founder, fresh from a sensitive week of feeling 'ganged up on' by the public, wasn't having it. While she described Lawrence's eventual apology email as 'so nice,' the damage was done—and the gloves were off.
